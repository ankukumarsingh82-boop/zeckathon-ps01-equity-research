       STRONG PERFORMANCE DRIVEN BY BROAD-BASED REVENUE GROWTH AND MARGIN
                                    EXPANSION
Mumbai, October 26, 2026: KFin Technologies Limited announced its financial results for the quarter and half year ended 30th September
2026 today.

Financial Highlights – Q2FY27
− Revenue from operations stood at ₹ 4018.0 million, up 29.9% y-o-y;
− International and other investor solutions revenue up by 157.9% y-o-y; (20.9% y-o-y excl. Ascent); Ascent Fund Services revenue
     up by 31.3% y-o-y
− EBITDA stood at ₹ 1,610.3 million, up 18.7% y-o-y, EBITDA margin excl. Ascent at 45.8% and incl. Ascent at 40.1%
− PAT at ₹ 1003.4 million, grew by 7.5% y-o-y, PAT margin excl. Ascent at 30.5% and incl. Ascent at 25.0%
− Diluted EPS stood at ₹ 5.78, grew by 7.5% y-o-y
− Non-domestic mutual fund revenue share in overall revenue improved to 42.1% y-o-y
Financial Highlights – H1FY27
− Revenue from operations stood at ₹ 7,583.3 million, up 30.0% y-o-y;
− International and other investor solutions revenue up by 168.8% y-o-y; (24.3% y-o-y excl. Ascent);
− EBITDA stood at ₹ 2,830.1 million, up 13.4% y-o-y, EBITDA margin excl. Ascent at 42.8% and incl. Ascent at 37.3%
− PAT at ₹ 1,755.6 million, grew by 2.9% y-o-y, PAT margin excl. Ascent at 28.7% and incl. Ascent at 23.2%
− Diluted EPS stood at ₹ 10.12, grew by 3.0% y-o-y
− Non-domestic mutual fund revenue share in overall revenue improved to 41.0% y-o-y
− Cash and Cash equivalents stood at ₹ 5,508.6 as on September 30, 2026
Business Highlights – Q2FY27
− Overall AAUM1 continues to grow faster than the industry; KFintech’s AAUM growth at 15.1% y-o-y vs. 13.1% for the industry; Overall
     AAUM market share at 33.1%; Equity AAUM1 growth at 14.3% y-o-y vs. 15.0% for the industry; Equity AAUM market share at 32.8%;
     Won a new RTA deal from Ashika Group;
− Number of international clients increased to 515 (Ascent: 394 and KFintech SEA: 121); AAUM increased y-o-y to US$51.9 billion
     (₹4,943 billion) from US$10.6 billion (₹934 billion); Won four new mandates for fund administration in GIFT city; Singapore, the
     USA, and the Middle East remain Ascent’s fastest-growing markets, with 24 new funds added in SG, 10 in the US, and 7 in the ME
− Added 437 new corporate clients under issuer solutions; Market share2 in NSE500 companies at 50.2%; New IPO mandates won
     during the quarter includes Abakkus Asset Manager, Mahanadi Coalfields, JSW One platform, Goldi Solar, Muthoot FinCorp etc
− No of alternate funds at 785; Market share at 37.2%; AUM grew 12.9% y-o-y to ₹ 20.3 trillion; Won 25 new AIF funds including
     from JM Financial Asset Management, 360 One WAM, Godrej Asset Management, Onterra Capital Advisor; Won a new mandate
     from a pension fund for end-to-end mPower platform solution
− NPS subscriber base grew to 2.5 mm by 41.2% Y-o-Y vs 14.1% Y-o-Y growth for the industry; Market share in overall subscribers’
     base at 12.8% as on September 30, 2026, up from 10.3% as on June 30, 2025

Commenting on the company’s performance, Sreekanth Nadella, Managing Director and CEO, KFin Technologies Limited said, "Our
financial results for the second quarter reflect KFintech’s resilience, product innovation capabilities and AI driven execution excellence
across the operating business lines. Revenues grew 30% y-o-y, led by strong deal wins across segments and the growing scale of our
international business albeit shallow momentum in the domestic public markets. EBITDA has expanded sequentially, driven by scale
benefits in non-mutual fund operating lines, deployment of AI and RPA driven productivity levers and disciplined cost management.We
have also augmented the sales pipeline substantively which will augur well for the coming quarters. Furthering with our vision to create
a global fund administrator from India, we deployed several industry first solutions - same-day SIP unitization, deployment of several
impactful mutual fund modules via Finex, Klarity - identity authentically platform, an enterprise-grade agentic Al solution to eliminate
signature fraud in the BFSI sector, introduced ARYA.AI - first of its kind conversational intelligence and investor servicing platform, Niti -
DPDP platforms amongst others. As we move into the second half of fiscal 2027, we remain focused on disciplined execution, scaling our
international business, and investing in Al-led platforms to drive sustained, long-term value for our shareholders."

About KFin Technologies Limited (www.kfintech.com/; BSE: 543720; NSE: KFINTECH):
KFin Technologies Limited (“KFintech”) is a leading technology driven financial services platform providing comprehensive services and
solutions to the capital markets ecosystem including asset managers and corporate issuers across asset classes in India and provide
comprehensive investor solutions including transfer agency, fund administration, fund accounting, data analytics, digital onboarding,
transaction origination and processing for alternate investments, mutual funds, private market funds, digital assets, unit trusts, insurance
investments, and private retirement schemes to global asset managers across 18 jurisdictions. In India, KFintech is the largest investor
solutions provider to Indian mutual funds, based on number of AMCs serviced as on September 30, 2026, and the largest issuer solutions
provider based on number of clients serviced as on September 30, 2026. KFintech is the only investor and issuer solutions provider in
India that offers services to asset managers such as mutual funds, alternative investment funds, wealth managers and pension as well as
corporate issuers and is one of the three operating central record keeping agencies for the National Pension System in India. KFintech is
listed on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited and BSE Limited. General Atlantic Singapore Fund Pte Ltd (“GASF”), a leading global
private equity investor, is the promoter of the company.
